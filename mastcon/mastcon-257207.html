  The Norton Guides are a marvelous vehicle for providing instant on-line
  help for programming languages.  However, the real power of the Guides
  lies in the ability to create your own reference databases.  The possib-
  ilities the Guides open for the imaginative user is virtually limitless.

  Master Conversions is the first Norton Guides reference database to be
  offered by Redshift Computing.  It contains what is possibly the most
  complete library of conversions available today for your personal computer.
  The conversion factors as contained in the Master Conversions database have
  been collected from a wide variety of U.S. Government documents, IUPAC and
  industry publications.

  Master Conversions is a copyrighted software product of Redshift Computing.
  Version 1.0 is a subset of Version 1.1.  It is being distributed as a
  shareware product.  Version 1.1 and all future versions will be made avail-
  able only to registered users.
