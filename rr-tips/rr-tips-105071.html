 Why did I made this Norton Guide?                  March 7, 1993

 I have to deal with a lot of questions regarding the use of R&R. These
 questions arise from the fact that I have included R&R runtime as the
 primary and sole to generate reports from my Clipper applications, all
 Sales and marketing productivity systems.

 Based upon this use, I train my users to develop their own reports. This
 seems to be succesful. However, it seems that the same questions arise
 agian and again. Following the R&R discussions and questions on
 CompuServe, the same applies.

 That's the reason why I have made this Norton Guide. Use it as you like,
 distribute is freely and please give me some feedback if you have
 anything to add.
